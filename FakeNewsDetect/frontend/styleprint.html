Stylistic Fingerprint Instrument · No. 1
STYLEPRINT
Reads the writing style of a news article and compares it against patterns learned from ~39,000 labeled articles. It detects how something is written, not whether it's true — see the fine print below.

◉ Paste text and press Analyze Signal to read its stylistic fingerprint.
What this actually measures: word and phrase patterns (TF-IDF over unigrams/bigrams) fed into a logistic regression classifier, trained on 2016–2017 U.S. political news where REAL articles are Reuters wire copy and FAKE articles come from a handful of hyperpartisan sites. It is picking up on writing register (formal wire-style vs. casual/opinionated), not verifying facts. On articles from unfamiliar sources it has ~93% accuracy and can be fooled by tone alone — the "Neutral claim" example above is included on purpose to show this. Text you paste is sent to the STYLEPRINT backend running at for scoring — not to any third party — and is not stored.
